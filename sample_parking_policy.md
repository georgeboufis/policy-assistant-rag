# Parking Policy

## 1. Who can park on site
The office car park has forty spaces. Spaces are assigned to employees who commute more
than twenty kilometres, and to anyone with a mobility need, on request to Facilities.

## 2. Electric vehicles
Six spaces have chargers. Charging is free for the first four hours, after which a
charge of two euros per hour applies, billed monthly through payroll.

## 3. Visitors
Visitor spaces must be booked at least one working day in advance through reception.
Unbooked visitor cars may be asked to leave.
