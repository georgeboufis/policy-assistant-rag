# Travel Policy

## 1. When to travel

Travel only when meeting in person clearly adds something a video call cannot. Internal meetings are held on video by default. Client visits, workshops and site inspections are good reasons to travel.

## 2. Booking

All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the corporate travel tool at least fourteen days before departure where possible. Bookings made less than seven days ahead need a one-line reason in the tool.

## 3. Class of travel

Economy class for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or more. Business class needs written approval from a director and is only granted for overnight flights followed by a working day.

Trains are booked in standard class. First class is allowed when it costs less than standard, which sometimes happens on advance fares.

## 4. Hotels

Hotels must be within the nightly limit shown in the travel tool for that city. If every hotel near the client is above the limit, choose the cheapest and add a note. Private rentals are not allowed because the company cannot guarantee their safety.

## 5. Safety

Register every trip in the travel tool so the company can find you in an emergency. For countries marked high risk, a security briefing is required before booking and travel needs approval from the security team.

## 6. Personal time

You may add personal days before or after a business trip. Any extra cost, including a more expensive return flight, is yours to pay.
