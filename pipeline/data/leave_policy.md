# Leave Policy

## 1. Annual leave

Full-time employees get twenty-five days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees get the same amount scaled to their working hours.

## 2. Booking leave

Request leave in the HR system and wait for your manager to approve it before booking anything. Requests for more than ten consecutive working days need at least one month's notice.

## 3. Carrying over

Up to five unused days can be carried into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by the end of March or they are lost. Unused days beyond five are not paid out, except when you leave the company.

## 4. Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before your normal start time. For absences of more than three consecutive working days, a doctor's note is required. Sick leave does not reduce your annual leave.

## 5. Parental leave

Birth parents get twenty weeks of paid leave. Other parents, including adoptive parents, get eight weeks of paid leave, which can be taken at any point in the first year. Tell HR at least eight weeks before you plan to start.

## 6. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to three months can be agreed with your manager and HR. It does not break your continuous employment, but annual leave does not build up while you are away.
