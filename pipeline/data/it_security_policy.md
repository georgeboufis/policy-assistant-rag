# IT Security Policy

## 1. Accounts and passwords

Every account uses a password of at least fourteen characters, and multi-factor authentication is switched on for all company systems. Never share a password, including with IT staff. IT will never ask you for it.

## 2. Devices

Only company laptops may connect to internal systems. Keep the operating system updated: updates are installed automatically, and a laptop that has missed updates for more than fourteen days is blocked from the network until it catches up.

USB storage devices are blocked. Use the company file-sharing service to move files instead.

## 3. Handling data

Client data stays on company systems. Do not paste client data into personal accounts, personal email, or public AI tools. Approved AI tools are listed on the intranet, and only those may be used with client information.

## 4. Reporting incidents

Report a lost or stolen device, a suspicious email you clicked, or any suspected breach to the security desk within one hour of noticing. Reporting quickly matters more than being sure. Nobody is disciplined for reporting their own mistake promptly.

## 5. Leaving the company

On your last day, return every device and access card. Your accounts are disabled at the end of that day, and personal files left on company systems are deleted after thirty days.
