New Starter Guide

Welcome. This page covers your first weeks: what to bring, what to set up, and who to ask.

Your first day

Come to the IT desk on the ground floor at 9:00 to collect your laptop and security key.

Bring photo ID. Without it, IT cannot hand over any equipment.

Setting up your accounts

You will receive an activation email for each company system. Activate every account within 48 hours, or the invitation expires and IT has to issue a new one.

Set up multi-factor authentication before you do anything else.

Required training

  • Security awareness training must be completed within your first two weeks.
  • Data protection training must be completed within your first month.
  • Both are online and take about an hour each.

Your buddy

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from their team for the first three months. Your buddy is your first stop for questions that are too small for your manager.
