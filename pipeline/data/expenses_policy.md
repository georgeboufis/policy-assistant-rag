# Expenses Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses the company pays for, how to claim them, and who approves them. It applies to all employees and to contractors whose contract says so.

## 2. What can be claimed

You can claim costs you paid personally while doing company work: travel, accommodation, meals while travelling, client hospitality, and small equipment needed for a specific task. Everyday commuting to your usual office cannot be claimed.

## 3. How to claim

Submit every claim through the expenses tool within thirty days of the date on the receipt. Claims submitted later than ninety days are rejected unless your line manager confirms in writing that the delay was unavoidable. Group claims are not allowed: each person claims their own costs.

## 4. Expenses

4.1 Travel is booked through the corporate tool. Tickets bought elsewhere are only refunded if the corporate tool had no availability, and you must attach a screenshot showing that.

4.2 Receipts are required above EUR 25. Below that amount a short written description is enough. A card statement is not a receipt.

4.3 Meals while travelling are covered up to EUR 60 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed, including at client dinners.

## 5. Approvals

5.1 Line managers approve up to EUR 5,000.

5.2 Anything above EUR 5,000 also needs approval from the finance director before the money is spent, not after.

5.3 You cannot approve your own claim, or a claim from someone who approves yours.

## 6. Mistakes and misuse

Honest mistakes are corrected by adjusting the next claim. Deliberately false claims are treated as misconduct and can lead to dismissal.
